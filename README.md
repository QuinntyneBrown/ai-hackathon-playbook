# AI Hackathon Playbook

Field-tested workflows for running large hackathons where teams build with AI-assisted development tools.

## Overview

This repository captures learnings from facilitating hackathon challenges delivered in collaboration with partner organizations. Each learning is recorded as a playbook: a repeatable workflow with named phases, numbered steps, and the rationale observed in practice. The playbooks target facilitators who lead teams of participants through a timeboxed build and intend to reapply the same structure at future events.

| Playbook | Setting | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [One-day team challenge](playbooks/one-day-team-challenge.md) | Team of 8–10 participants, challenge supplied by a partner organization, AI-assisted development | One hack day |

## Background

The ideation phases in these playbooks reference the [FaithTech 4D cycle](https://workbook.faithtech.com/) — Discover, Discern, Develop, Demonstrate. The Discover output, gathered with or supplied by the partner organization, is the input to team brainstorming.
